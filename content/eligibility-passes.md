# Student, Teacher, and Military Passes

## What it is

The **America the Beautiful – National Parks & Federal Recreational Lands Pass** program is the umbrella for almost every pass federal recreation lands sell or give, and for a park "free family year" program aimed at fourth graders. This section covers the passes that are **free or worth knowing about**, organized by who qualifies.

Official source for everything in this section: <https://store.usgs.gov/pass> and <https://www.everykidoutdoors.gov/>

---

## Every Kid Outdoors — fourth graders (free annual family pass)

**What it is:** Every U.S. fourth grader (including home-schooled and free-choice learners) can get an **annual pass that covers free entrance for the fourth grader and all accompanying family members in the same vehicle** to every national park and national wildlife refuge or waterfowl site in the federal system.

**Eligibility:**

- Any child in the **fourth-grade school year** (during the school year and through the following August 31).
- Covers the child's whole family **in one family vehicle** (the fourth-grade pass covers the car, not per-person; big commercial buses and tour groups don't count).
- Second language is a non-issue: the pass page works via the "Get Your Pass" form; you print an exchange coupon/voucher and pick up a hard pass at participating sites.

**Check availability:** <https://everykidoutdoors.gov/index.htm>

**Fine print:** The pass requires completing a short activity on the child's own official webpage and printing a voucher — free, no fees, no credit card. Vouchers often need to be exchanged for a physical pass at participating federal gateways.

## America the Beautiful passes worth owning (free for most veterans and disabilities)

There are several pass types sold or given under one brand. Here's the cheat sheet:

| Pass | Cost | Who qualifies | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Military Annual Pass** | **Free** | Currently serving U.S. military members and their dependents (with DoD ID) | <https://store.usgs.gov/pass> |
| **Gold Star Family Free Pass** | **Free, lifetime** | Families of fallen service members (with Gold Star documentation/VetRec forms) | <https://store.usgs.gov/pass> |
| **Access Pass** | **Free, lifetime** | U.S. citizens and permanent residents with **permanent disabilities**, any age | <https://store.usgs.gov/pass> |
| **Volunteer Pass** | **Free, annual** | Volunteers with ≥250 service hours with federal agencies | <https://store.usgs.gov/pass> |
| **Senior Annual Pass** | $20/yr | U.S. citizens/permanent residents **62+** | <https://store.usgs.gov/pass> |
| **Senior Lifetime Pass** | $80 | U.S. citizens/permanent residents **62+** | <https://store.usgs.gov/pass> |
| **Annual Pass (everyone)** | $80/yr | Anyone | <https://store.usgs.gov/pass> |
| **International annual (non-resident)** | varies | International visitors (new 2026 policy at 11 flagship parks) | <https://www.recreation.gov/> |

What those free passes do: waive the **entrance fee** to national parks and wildlife refuges, and often halve or eliminate fees on federal lands (camping discounts are typical, 50% at most participating sites where fees still apply). Senior and Access passes also give standard 50% discounts on most amenity fees including camping.

**Check availability:** <https://store.usgs.gov/pass> (USGS is the official distributor).

## What teachers actually get (honest version)

There is no national free "teacher park pass," and anyone saying otherwise is selling something. What teachers really can use, verified:

- **Teacher discounts at museums:** most large museums have a teacher-discount line (commonly 10–25% off, sometimes free on teacher days, often with free educator memberships to science museums). Check each museum's education page — educators usually need a school ID or a teacher paystub, and **many museums give teachers free admission outright on requested educator-visit days**.
- **Educator perks apply everywhere:** one example of the pattern: the Exploratorium in San Francisco gives **free admission to California public-school K–12 teachers** (annual voucher program) plus free field trips for Title I schools — <https://www.exploratorium.edu/education/exploratorium-for-educators>. Similar teacher-free or teacher-discount policies exist at many science museums and zoos; check each museum's education page.
- **Every Kid Outdoors by classroom:** teachers of fourth-grade classrooms can arrange **free ranger-led programs and field trips** at many NPS sites; many will waive the entry fees for the whole bus. Start at the park's "education" page via <https://www.nps.gov/findapark/index.htm>.
- **Teacher-Ranger-Teacher (TRT) program:** some NPS parks pay selected teachers to work as park rangers over the summer (paid professional development that also unlocks park access). Applications are made through individual parks each spring: <https://www.nps.gov/subjects/teachers/index.htm>.

**Check availability:** your own park or museum's education page; the NPS teacher hub is <https://www.nps.gov/subjects/teachers/index.htm>.

## Military and veteran summary

- **Active duty + dependents:** free annual Military Pass every year.
- **Gold Star families:** free lifetime pass.
- **Veterans:** since 2021, U.S. veterans get awarded **free copies of the America the Beautiful pass at participating sites** — the VA's VetRec system and participating federal entry desks are the issuing channels; see <https://www.va.gov/history/vetrec/>.
- **Disabled veterans:** the Access Pass (free, lifetime) is available to anyone with a permanent service-connected disability rating — <https://store.usgs.gov/pass>.
- **Blue Star Museums** for free summer museum visits (see the [free museums section](free-museums.html)).

## Fine print to know

- America the Beautiful free passes are **non-transferable**: signed, photo-checked at the gate; one pass per eligible person.
- The passes **don't include** camping, tours, special permits, or state-park privileges — states run their own systems (see [state parks section](state-parks.html)).
- Fourth-grade households: the pass covers only the **family vehicle itself**, so big family reunions with three cars need three passes or a fee day.
